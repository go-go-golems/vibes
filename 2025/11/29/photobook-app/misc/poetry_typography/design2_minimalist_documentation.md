# Design 2: Minimalist with Extreme Whitespace

This design takes a contemporary and minimalist approach to poetry typography. The focus is on creating a sense of quiet contemplation and allowing the words to resonate in a vast, open space. The aesthetic is clean, uncluttered, and highly modern.

## Typographic Approach

The guiding principle of this design is **"less is more."** The layout is characterized by extreme whitespace, with very generous margins and line spacing. This creates a strong visual contrast between the text and the surrounding emptiness, drawing the reader's attention to the poem itself. The poem is presented as a floating object in a sea of white, encouraging a focused and immersive reading experience.

## Font Selection

The chosen typeface is **Linux Libertine**, a modern and elegant open-source serif font. Its clean lines and balanced proportions make it an excellent choice for a minimalist design. It is highly readable and has a subtle sophistication that complements the contemporary aesthetic without being distracting.

## Whitespace and Layout

Whitespace is the dominant element of this design. The margins are exceptionally large, and the line spacing is set to 1.8 to create a sense of airiness and separation between the lines. The layout is simple and asymmetrical, with the poem flush-left, which adds a touch of modernity and dynamism. The title page is also designed with a minimalist aesthetic, using simple, clean typography and ample whitespace.

## Sources and Inspiration

- **Minimalist Design Principles:** The design is inspired by the principles of minimalist graphic design, which emphasize simplicity, clarity, and the use of negative space. [1]
- **Contemporary Poetry Layouts:** The use of extreme whitespace and asymmetrical layouts is influenced by contemporary trends in poetry book design, which often prioritize a clean and modern aesthetic. [2]
- **Photobook Design:** The concept of using whitespace to create a contemplative and immersive experience is also borrowed from the world of fine art photobook design, where the arrangement of images and text is carefully considered to create a specific mood and narrative. [3]
