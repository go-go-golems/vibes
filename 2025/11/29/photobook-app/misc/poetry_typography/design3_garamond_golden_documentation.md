# Design 3: Garamond with Golden Ratio Proportions

This design seeks to achieve a sense of Renaissance elegance and mathematical harmony. It combines a classic typeface with a layout based on the golden ratio, resulting in a design that is both beautiful and intellectually satisfying.

## Typographic Approach

The layout is based on the **golden ratio (approximately 1:1.618)**, a proportion that has been used in art, architecture, and design for centuries to create aesthetically pleasing compositions. The margins are set according to this ratio, creating a sense of balance and harmony that is both subtle and profound. The use of the `memoir` class in LaTeX allows for precise control over the page layout, ensuring that the golden ratio is applied accurately.

## Font Selection

The chosen typeface is **EB Garamond**, an open-source revival of the classic Garamond typeface. Garamond is a quintessential Renaissance typeface, known for its elegance, readability, and timeless beauty. Its calligraphic roots and gentle curves give it a warm and inviting feel, making it an excellent choice for a classic poetry layout.

## Whitespace and Layout

The use of the golden ratio in the margins creates a dynamic and visually interesting page. The whitespace is generous but not excessive, creating a sense of openness without sacrificing the intimacy of the reading experience. The layout is traditional and centered, with the poem flowing gracefully down the page.

## Sources and Inspiration

- **Golden Ratio in Design:** The use of the golden ratio is inspired by its long history in art and design as a tool for creating harmonious and aesthetically pleasing compositions. [1]
- **Garamond Typeface:** The choice of Garamond is influenced by its historical significance and its reputation as one of the most beautiful and readable typefaces ever created. [2]
- **Memoir Class for LaTeX:** The `memoir` class was chosen for its powerful and flexible tools for page layout and design, which are ideal for creating a custom layout based on the golden ratio. [3]
